# Product Roadmap

## 🏢 Enterprise Features

- [ ] **White-Label & Vanity Domains** 
- [ ] **Enterprise Description:** Establish brand trust and eliminate third-party signaling by hosting forms on your own subdomains (e.g., `forms.yourorg.org`).

- **The Value:** Fully managed SSL termination and custom DNS routing ensure that respondents never leave your security perimeter, increasing completion rates by maintaining a seamless brand experience.

### 2. Design System & Brand Alignment

- **Enterprise Description:** Granular control over CSS variables, including specialized color palettes, typography, and logo placement.
- **The Value:** Ensure every form meets strict corporate identity (CI) guidelines. Support for "Dark Mode" and high-contrast accessibility themes ensures compliance with global WCAG standards.

### 3. Privacy-Preserving Telemetry & Analytics

- **Enterprise Description:** High-fidelity insight into form performance without compromising respondent anonymity.
- **The Value:** Gain "Zero-Knowledge" metrics on drop-off points and device distribution. All analytics are aggregated and anonymized at the edge, ensuring you meet the most stringent GDPR and HIPAA data-minimization requirements.

### 4. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Team Workspaces

- **Enterprise Description:** Robust organizational hierarchies with defined roles (Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer).
- **The Value:** Securely manage form ownership across departments. Enable cross-functional collaboration with "Team Workspaces" while maintaining strict "Least Privilege" access to sensitive encrypted response data.

### 5. Persistent Session & Partial Submissions

- **Enterprise Description:** Support for asynchronous data entry through encrypted "Save & Continue" functionality.
- **The Value:** Reduce attrition on complex, multi-page forms by allowing respondents to securely cache progress locally. Organizations can analyze partial completion trends to optimize form flow and capture intent before a final submission
